Official Player Support and Account Deletion

Delete Your Account

Players of Wanted: Wild Run can request permanent deletion of their game account and associated data, even if they have uninstalled the game.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Click the Request Account Deletion button below.
  2. Provide the email address linked to your game account.
  3. Confirm that you want your account and associated data deleted.
  4. Send your request to our support team.
Request Account Deletion

Data Covered by Account Deletion

Account deletion covers associated personal and game data, including:

  • Game account information.
  • Email address and account profile.
  • Cloud-saved game progress.
  • Account-linked coins and gems.
  • Unlocked characters and achievements.
  • Account-linked leaderboard records.

Data Retention

Some transaction or security records may need to be retained for legitimate legal, accounting, security or fraud-prevention purposes. Contact support for information about applicable retention periods.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and may result in the loss of saved progress, characters and virtual items. We may verify account ownership before processing your request. Never send your password or verification codes.
